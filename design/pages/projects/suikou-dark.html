SProjects: state storyboard

The Suikou Projects page rendered as a state storyboard in Suikou Dark, the same near-black cool-tinted dev workbench as the Review page. It is the app home and launcher: a two-column shell with a project sidebar on the left and the selected project's review list on the right, under a minimal toolbar carrying the Suikou seal, a search hint, a primary + New, and display options. Reviews come in two kinds, shown with monochrome line glyphs, never color emoji: file_selection (a document glyph, "N files") and git_diff (a compare glyph, a base..head refs hint). Selection and hover are full background fills with a hairline ring, never side stripes. Unread reviews carry an accent dot at the row start; approved reviews carry an approve-green check + tag; open blockers show a small request-red count. OKLCH only, amber for numbers, zero em dashes.
