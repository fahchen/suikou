SProjects: phone storyboard

The Suikou Projects page (app home and launcher) rendered as a phone state storyboard in Suikou Light, the lit twin of the dark desktop launcher in ./suikou-dark.html. On a phone the desktop two-column split collapses to one column: the project sidebar becomes a horizontal scrollable chip strip (selected project is a full accent fill with a hairline ring, never a side stripe), and the selected project's review list runs full width below it. Reviews come in two kinds shown with monochrome line glyphs, never color emoji: file_selection (a document glyph, N files) and git_diff (a compare glyph, a mono base..head refs hint). Unread reviews carry an accent dot; approved reviews carry an approve-green check; each row ends in a chevron. Creation flows are iOS bottom sheets. OKLCH only, amber for numbers, zero em dashes.
