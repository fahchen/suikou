SProjects: phone layout

The responsive phone face of the Suikou Projects page, the same app as the Review storyboard (../review/states-codex.html), wearing one Suikou Dark skin: the same near-black cool-tinted charcoal over a faint teal/blue desk glow, dark glass slabs with hairline rings, a cyan to teal to blue gradient spent only on the primary action, amber for numbers and refs. The desktop two-column board (project sidebar + review list) collapses into a single column: a slim app bar, a horizontally scrollable project chip strip standing in for the sidebar, the selected project header with its path, a full-width + New review, and full-width tappable review rows. Each review carries a monochrome kind glyph (FileText for file_selection, GitCompare for git_diff), never a color emoji. Selection and active state use a full background fill plus a hairline ring, never a side stripe. Tap targets and rows are sized for fingers (~60px) while keeping the dense, expert character. Creation flows are bottom sheets over a dimmed board.
