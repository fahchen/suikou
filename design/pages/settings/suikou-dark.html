SSettings: state storyboard

The Suikou Settings surface in the Suikou Dark direction. Settings is not a route: it opens with ⌘, as a modal centered over the dimmed projects board on desktop, and as a full-screen sheet on phone, and closes back to wherever you were. This is a single-user, local tool, so there are no accounts; the modal has a left section rail (Appearance, Review defaults, Agent, Keyboard, About) and a right content pane, like Linear or macOS System Settings. Every change applies live (no Save / Cancel), so the footer carries only a primary Done. Each control row is a label on the left and its control on the right, with a one-line sub-label where it helps. Same near-black charcoal over a faint teal/blue glow, dark glass panels with hairline rings, a cyan to teal to blue accent spent only on the active segment, the primary button, and the focus ring.

Settings sheet (phone) product
On phone the modal becomes a full-screen sheet: a top bar with Settings + Done, then the sections as a grouped scrolling list (iOS-settings style rows). Appearance and Review groups show their controls inline; segmented controls and toggles are sized for touch (~44px hit area), with wide segmented controls stacked under their label where they would crowd.
