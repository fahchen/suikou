SReview: state storyboard

The Suikou Review page rendered as a full-page state storyboard in the Suikou Dark direction. The right pane is not a persistent inspector: almost every page is two columns (navigator + editor) with the comment thread shown full and inline at its line, and the review roll-up reachable from a toolbar Review popover. Only the side rail page keeps a third column, and the all files page is a real stacked two-column layout. Near-black charcoal over a faint teal/blue glow, dark glass panels with thin hairline borders, a cyan to teal to blue gradient accent spent sparingly plus amber for numbers, green/red diff rows, monospace syntax-highlighted code, and terminal idioms (>_ command pills and Applied notes on agent replies). The storyboard runs in two parts: Part 1 is a file_selection review (review these files as they are now); Part 2 is a git_diff review (a diff of two refs, with per-file +N / −M stats, a native diff editor, and diff_hunk comments). Each page is preceded by a labeled divider carrying its sequence number, name, and the states.md catalog ID.

Comment layout: side rail (crowded) E14 H1
The mutually-exclusive alternative to the inline thread. Comments leave the editor and float in a right-side rail, each card vertically aligned to its anchored code line (Notion style); the code line keeps only a small marker dot, no inline card. When a rail gets crowded the cards fold to save room: a 1-line card (the densest state) shows only the type pill plus a single-line comment summary, no reply; one click takes it to the normal collapsed card (comment plus latest reply, each clamped to 3 lines); another click focuses it for the full reply list and hidden text. Here six comments share the rail: four are folded to one line, line 17 sits at the normal 3-line collapse, and line 13 is focused / expanded.
