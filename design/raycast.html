Suikou, as Raycast

A style exploration that reframes the Suikou code-review tool as a Raycast-style command app. One floating, translucent window over a blurred wallpaper. Search, files, verdicts, comments, and rounds all driven by a command list, a split detail view, an action panel, and keyboard shortcuts.
